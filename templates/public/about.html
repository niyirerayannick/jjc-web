Who We Are

About Jehovah Jireh Choir

A spirit-filled gospel choir from the University of Lay Catholics of Kigali, dedicated to worship and ministry.

Our Story

Jehovah Jireh Choir – ULK was founded by students of the University of Lay Catholics of Kigali (ULK) with a burning desire to use music as a tool for evangelization, worship, and community transformation. The name "Jehovah Jireh" — meaning "The Lord Will Provide" — reflects our deep trust in God's provision as we go about our ministry.

Since our founding, the choir has grown into one of Rwanda's most recognized university gospel choirs, known for powerful worship performances, community outreach programs, and unwavering commitment to sharing the gospel through music.

Our Values

  • Excellence in Worship — We pursue the highest standard in our musical ministry as an offering to God.
  • Evangelization — We take the gospel beyond the church walls, to campuses, communities, and prisons.
  • Unity — We celebrate our diversity as members and believe in the power of collective worship.
  • Service — We exist to serve God, our university, and the broader community.
  • Integrity — We model Christ-like character in everything we do.
